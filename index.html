Burgers at the pier, back every spring.

West Pier Drive-In is a Soo summer institution — a drive-in burgers restaurant on W Pier Dr, on the St. Marys River side of Sault Ste. Marie. When the season opens each spring, the pier fills up again: cars in, burgers out, and the river right there beside you.
